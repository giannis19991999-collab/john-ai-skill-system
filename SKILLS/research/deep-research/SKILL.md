# Deep Research

1. Translate the request into a decision question.
2. Break it into answerable subquestions.
3. Search primary sources first: official documentation, filings, product pages, regulatory sources, supplier specifications.
4. Add credible secondary sources and community discussions where useful.
5. Cross-check important claims independently.
6. Record dates for time-sensitive facts.
7. Separate evidence from interpretation.
8. Synthesize around the user's decision.

## Output
- Main finding
- Key evidence and citations
- Competitor or market patterns
- Unknowns and limitations
- Practical implications
- Recommended next test

Check source freshness, context, and whether each source actually supports the claim.
