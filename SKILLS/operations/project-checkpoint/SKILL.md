# Project Checkpoint

At the end of substantial work, record:

## Project and objective
Name and intended outcome.

## Current state
Completed and incomplete work.

## Decisions
Decision, rationale, date, and reversibility.

## Constraints
Budget, tools, deadlines, market, quality requirements.

## Evidence
Metrics, sources, files, and observations. Separate measured data from estimates.

## Open questions
Only unresolved issues that materially affect the next step.

## Next action
One concrete action and its success condition.

Do not silently overwrite old decisions. Mark superseded decisions and explain why.
