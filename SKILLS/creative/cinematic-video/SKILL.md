# Cinematic Product Video

## Pre-production
1. Identify real product features and visual details that must not change.
2. Define audience, emotional trigger, setting, and one-sentence story.
3. Choose a format: reveal, unboxing, reaction, problem-solution, or lifestyle integration.
4. Build a shot list with duration, framing, camera movement, action, product visibility, and transition.
5. Lock continuity: product geometry, packaging, colors, room, actors, props, and lighting.

## Every shot must specify
- Shot size: wide, medium, close-up, macro.
- Angle: eye-level, low, high, POV, over-the-shoulder.
- Movement: push-in, pull-back, pan, tilt, orbit, tracking, handheld.
- Subject action and product position.
- Lighting, background, and start/end-frame continuity.

## Generation discipline
- Use product reference images where supported.
- Do not ask a model to invent critical product details.
- Keep each clip's action simple.
- For a continuous shot, explicitly require one uninterrupted camera move and prohibit cuts or scene changes.
- Inspect for warped text/logos, altered geometry, extra parts, bad hands, and impossible physics.
- Prototype one clip before building a five-videos-per-day pipeline.

## Deliverables
Concept, timed shot list, image prompts, video prompts, voiceover, sound design, editing instructions, and product-fidelity checklist.
