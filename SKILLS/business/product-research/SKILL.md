# Product Research

1. Define target country, customer, use case, and target price.
2. Identify the customer problem/desire and evidence people pay to solve it.
3. Review direct competitors, substitutes, positioning, offers, reviews, and complaints.
4. Extract customer language from reviews, forums, Reddit, and comments; treat anecdotes as signals, not market-size proof.
5. Verify supplier identity, specifications, shipping origin, delivery, tracking, returns, and quality evidence.
6. Identify legal, safety, intellectual-property, platform, and advertising-policy risks.
7. Assess differentiation that can be demonstrated honestly.
8. Recommend a low-cost validation test.

## Required output
- Target buyer and buying trigger
- Demand evidence
- Competitor map
- Customer objections
- Supplier and fulfillment risks
- Differentiation angle
- Validation plan with pass/fail criteria
- Go / Test / No-go recommendation

Never confuse social views with purchase intent. A supplier listing is not proof of quality or reliable fulfillment.
