# E-commerce Unit Economics

Collect or explicitly assume:
- Selling price
- Product cost, shipping, packaging
- Payment processing and currency conversion
- Platform fees
- Refunds, returns, chargebacks, fraud
- Duties, VAT, sales tax where applicable
- Discounts, commissions, acquisition cost
- Monthly fixed costs and order volume

Calculate:
- Contribution before acquisition = price minus variable costs excluding acquisition.
- Contribution after acquisition = contribution before acquisition minus CAC.
- Break-even orders = monthly fixed costs divided by contribution per order.

Show conservative, base, and optimistic cases. Make assumptions visible. Flag missing data. Never label revenue as profit.
